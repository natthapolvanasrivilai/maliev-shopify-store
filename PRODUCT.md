# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is small manufacturers, workshops, makers, and product developers who want credible injection-molding capability without the footprint or capital cost of traditional industrial equipment. They may be evaluating their first machine, moving prototypes into small-batch production, or bringing low-volume manufacturing in-house. They need clear technical information and enough confidence to schedule a demonstration, request a quotation, or ask MALIEV for practical advice.

## Product Purpose

The storefront introduces MALIEV's compact injection molding machines, explains how they make small-batch production more accessible, and turns qualified interest into demo and quotation conversations that lead to machine purchases. It should also make supporting products and services easy to discover without diluting the injection molding machine as the primary commercial focus.

Success means a visitor understands that the machine can fit a small workshop or office, believes it can perform serious production work, and feels comfortable contacting MALIEV to evaluate the right machine and mold solution.

## Positioning

MALIEV delivers serious, locally supported injection-molding capability in a compact and approachable system, with substantially lower machine and mold costs than traditional industrial equipment.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: schedule a machine demonstration or request a quotation.
- Secondary CTA: chat with MALIEV on LINE or download detailed machine specifications.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: a modern, easy-to-use, affordable injection molding machine made by a high-quality Thai manufacturer.
- Belief ladder:
  1. The machine fits a small workshop or office.
  2. It can deliver credible small-batch production.
  3. Its machine and mold costs are substantially more accessible than industrial alternatives.
  4. MALIEV manufactures the machine in-house and can maintain it locally.
  5. Replacement parts, mold-making assistance, and a one-year warranty reduce ownership risk.
  6. Scheduling a demonstration or requesting a quotation is the sensible next step.
- Proof on hand: accurate KeyShot and Blender product renders; the in-house manufacturing story; local Thai support; replacement-part availability; mold-making assistance; and a one-year machine warranty. Customer-owned molds and parts must not be shown without permission. No customer logos or testimonials are approved for use yet. Product imagery should follow [IMAGE_GUIDELINES.md](IMAGE_GUIDELINES.md).

## Brand Personality

MALIEV is ingenious, dependable, and approachable: smart, practical engineering that makes industrial capability accessible; high-quality in-house manufacturing backed by local support and available parts; and a team that is easy to understand and comfortable to contact for advice or a demonstration. The emotional promise is serious engineering without the intimidation or overhead of traditional industrial machinery.

## Anti-references

The storefront must not look or behave like a lightly customized Shopify template, especially the recognizable default Dawn theme. It should also avoid generic industrial-supplier clutter and visual theatrics that obscure engineering facts, pricing, or the path to speaking with MALIEV.

## Design Principles

1. **Lead with the machine.** Injection molding equipment is the storefront's primary story; supporting products and services should reinforce rather than compete with it.
2. **Make engineering understandable.** Explain dimensions, capabilities, ownership requirements, pricing, and tradeoffs with the clarity of an excellent product launch.
3. **Prove compact capability.** Use accurate scale, realistic product imagery, technical detail, and demonstrations to show that a small footprint still delivers serious work.
4. **Turn local manufacturing into confidence.** Make in-house production, Thai support, replacement parts, mold-making assistance, and warranty coverage tangible throughout the journey.
5. **Earn the conversation.** Give visitors enough useful detail to self-qualify, then make demo, quotation, LINE, and specification actions obvious and low-friction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA across the responsive storefront. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, descriptive alternative text, semantic structure, and zoom-friendly layouts. Rich product-launch motion must have reduced-motion alternatives that communicate the same information without requiring animation. Do not rely on color or motion alone to explain machine states, differences, or calls to action.
