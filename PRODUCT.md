# Product

## Register

product

The storefront shell (home, collections, cart, checkout, account, search) is a functional store: clear catalog, honest prices, frictionless purchase. Each product line's detail and launch pages (Injection Molding Machines, SimMount) are treated as keynote-style product storytelling — the brand register applies there per task.

## Users

Two distinct audiences with different buying journeys:

1. **Machine buyers (Thailand).** Small manufacturers, workshops, makers, and product developers evaluating compact injection molding capability without industrial footprint or capital cost. This is a high-consideration purchase: every machine customer so far has visited the MALIEV factory to see the machine in person before committing. They need clear technical information, credible proof of capability, and a comfortable path to a factory visit or conversation.
2. **SimMount buyers (international).** Sim racing enthusiasts buying MALIEV's in-house-developed 3D-printed sim racing accessories. This is a standard e-commerce purchase: they evaluate fit and quality from the page and buy directly.

## Product Purpose

The storefront sells MALIEV's in-house products through two conversion tracks:

- **Machines:** introduce the compact injection molding machines, prove that a small footprint delivers serious production work, and convert qualified interest into a factory visit or demonstration. The store supports a 50% deposit checkout that starts machine production — positioned as the natural step after seeing the machine, not the cold first click. Quotation requests and LINE chat remain low-friction paths. Direct full purchase is possible but expected to be rare at this price point. Machines sell only within Thailand (no CE marking yet, which blocks import into most foreign markets); the store must scope this clearly without apologizing for it.
- **SimMount:** present the products with enough visual and technical confidence that international sim racers add to cart and check out directly.

Success means: a machine visitor believes the machine fits their workshop and books a visit or opens a conversation; a SimMount visitor completes checkout.

## Positioning

MALIEV delivers serious, locally supported injection-molding capability in a compact and approachable system, with substantially lower machine and mold costs than traditional industrial equipment. SimMount extends the same in-house engineering into precision accessories for the sim racing community.

## Conversion & proof

- **Machines — primary CTA:** book a factory visit / machine demonstration. **Supporting:** 50% production deposit checkout, request a quotation, chat on LINE, download specifications.
- **SimMount — primary CTA:** add to cart and check out.
- The line a machine visitor remembers after 10 seconds: a modern, easy-to-use, affordable injection molding machine made by a high-quality Thai manufacturer.
- Machine belief ladder:
  1. The machine fits a small workshop or office.
  2. It can deliver credible small-batch production.
  3. Its machine and mold costs are substantially more accessible than industrial alternatives.
  4. MALIEV manufactures the machine in-house and can maintain it locally.
  5. Replacement parts, mold-making assistance, and a one-year warranty reduce ownership risk.
  6. Visiting the factory to see the machine — then placing a 50% deposit — is the sensible next step.
- Proof on hand: accurate KeyShot and Blender product renders; the in-house manufacturing story; local Thai support; replacement-part availability; mold-making assistance; and a one-year machine warranty. Customer-owned molds and parts must not be shown without permission. No customer logos or testimonials are approved for use yet. ## Brand Personality

**MALIEV (parent, machines):** ingenious, dependable, approachable — smart, practical engineering that makes industrial capability accessible; high-quality in-house manufacturing backed by local support and available parts; a team that is easy to understand and comfortable to contact. The emotional promise is serious engineering without the intimidation or overhead of traditional industrial machinery.

**SimMount (sub-brand):** motorsport-technical — racing-paddock energy, telemetry-style precision, and speed cues, in the spirit of serious sim racing hardware brands (Fanatec, MOZA) without imitating them. SimMount lives inside the MALIEV store as a distinct visual world; the shared thread is precision engineering, not shared styling.

## Anti-references

- The storefront must not look or behave like a lightly customized Shopify template, especially the recognizable default Dawn theme.
- No generic industrial-supplier clutter, and no visual theatrics that obscure engineering facts, pricing, or the path to speaking with MALIEV.
- SimMount must not collapse into gamer-RGB clutter or aggressive esports styling; motorsport-technical means precision and speed, not neon noise.

## Design Principles

1. **Functional shell, keynote products.** The store's chrome (home, collections, cart, search) stays clean and transactional; each product line's pages earn Apple-launch-grade storytelling. Don't blur the two.
2. **Two sub-brands, one store.** MALIEV machines carry the engineering identity; SimMount carries a motorsport-technical identity. Imagery and per-line styling differentiate them; store structure and quality standards unify them.
3. **Make engineering understandable.** Explain dimensions, capabilities, ownership requirements, pricing, and tradeoffs with the clarity of an excellent product launch.
4. **Match friction to the purchase.** Machines earn a conversation and a factory visit before money moves; SimMount removes every step between interest and checkout. Never impose one track's friction on the other.
5. **Turn local manufacturing into confidence.** Make in-house production, Thai support, replacement parts, mold-making assistance, and warranty coverage tangible throughout the machine journey — and let the same in-house capability vouch for SimMount quality.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA across the responsive storefront. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, descriptive alternative text, semantic structure, and zoom-friendly layouts in both Thai and English. Rich product-launch motion must have reduced-motion alternatives that communicate the same information without requiring animation. Do not rely on color or motion alone to explain machine states, differences, or calls to action.
