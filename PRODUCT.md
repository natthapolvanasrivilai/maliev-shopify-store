# Product

## Register

product

## Product model

MALIEV is one manufacturer with two commerce journeys inside one store.

1. **Compact injection molding machines and manufacturing support (Thailand).** These are considered purchases. Visitors must understand fit, capability, tooling, service, and ownership before they act. The primary conversion is a factory demonstration or conversation; quotation and LINE are supporting paths. A 50% THB deposit can start production after qualification, but checkout must never be presented as the expected first interaction.
2. **SimMount products (international).** These are direct e-commerce purchases. Visitors need compatibility, product detail, delivery confidence, and a short path from product selection to checkout.

The wider catalog—molds, spare parts, 3D-printed products, Mesh Splitter, articles, and manufacturing services—supports those journeys without competing with them.

## Users

### Machine buyers

Thai manufacturers, workshops, makers, educators, and product developers evaluating small-batch plastic production. They may be technically capable but unfamiliar with injection molding ownership. They compare physical footprint, shot capacity, mold requirements, utilities, support, warranty, and total practical risk. Most qualified buyers want to see the real machine and speak with its manufacturer before committing.

### SimMount buyers

International sim racers and cockpit builders who already understand their rig. They compare fit, adjustability, included hardware, materials, availability, price, and shipping. They expect familiar direct-commerce behavior and should not inherit the friction of the machine journey.

### Existing owners and returning customers

Machine owners, SimMount customers, and workshop users returning for parts, documentation, compatible accessories, account history, or support. Compatibility and service paths must be as discoverable as new products.

## Product purpose

The store makes MALIEV's engineered products understandable, comparable, purchasable, and supportable.

Success means:

- a qualified machine visitor books a demo session, requests a quotation, or starts a useful LINE conversation;
- an informed machine buyer can place the correct 50% deposit in THB after qualification;
- a SimMount visitor identifies compatibility and completes checkout without avoidable friction;
- an existing owner finds the right document, part, or contact route quickly;
- every visitor can distinguish MALIEV-made products from services, tools, and supporting content.

## Positioning

MALIEV makes serious engineering accessible at workshop scale. The company designs, manufactures, and supports its own products in Thailand, with direct access to the people who build them.

The memorable machine claim is: **real injection molding capability, sized and supported for a Thai workshop.**

The memorable SimMount claim is: **precise rig hardware, engineered and manufactured by the same team that builds production equipment.**

## Personality

- **Exact:** dimensions, prices, compatibility, availability, and limitations are explicit.
- **Capable:** products are shown doing credible work, not surrounded by empty industrial theatre.
- **Approachable:** technical information is translated into decisions and direct next steps.
- **Responsible:** support, replacement parts, warranty, and ownership requirements are visible before purchase.

## Content and proof hierarchy

Use the strongest available evidence in this order:

1. Accurate product imagery and verified specifications.
2. In-house manufacturing and real factory processes.
3. Clear compatibility, service, warranty, and replacement-part commitments.
4. Transparent price, deposit, shipping, and availability information.
5. Useful guides and documentation.

Do not use customer-owned molds, parts, identities, logos, or claims without permission. Do not invent performance comparisons, savings percentages, production volumes, delivery promises, certifications, or testimonials.

## Store architecture

The custom theme serves the complete Online Store 2.0 surface:

- global announcement, header, navigation, predictive search, cart drawer, localization, and footer;
- homepage and product-family landing pages;
- collection, search, filtering, sorting, and product cards;
- machine, SimMount, standard, made-to-order, and campaign product templates;
- product media, variants, quantity, availability, price, deposit, and buy controls;
- cart, customer account, authentication, addresses, and order history;
- blog, article, contact, generic page, policies, documentation, and service content;
- password, gift card, empty, error, and 404 states.

The theme must not read as a modified Dawn installation. Shopify's commerce contracts remain, but MALIEV owns the structure, tokens, components, responsive behavior, and visual language.

## Design principles

1. **Organize by the decision, not by the theme primitive.** Pages explain what the visitor is choosing and why; sections and cards never become the visible information architecture.
2. **Show the engineered object first.** Product imagery, verified specifications, compatibility, and real construction carry more weight than decorative brand language.
3. **Match friction to purchase risk.** Machines lead to demonstration and conversation; SimMount leads to cart. Never force one journey onto the other.
4. **Make ownership part of the product.** Support, repair, documents, parts, warranty, shipping, and availability are visible throughout the journey.
5. **One system, distinct operating modes.** Machine, SimMount, commerce, and editorial pages share typography, spacing, components, and interaction rules while using controlled surface modes to express context.
6. **Thai-first clarity, international completeness.** Thai copy is native and useful rather than mechanically translated; English remains complete for international customers. Layouts tolerate both scripts without truncation or typographic compromise.
7. **Theme Editor without theme sameness.** Merchant-editable schemas, blocks, and localization are preserved, while the rendered experience remains unmistakably MALIEV.

## Anti-references

- A lightly reskinned Shopify Dawn theme or any storefront assembled from recognizable default section patterns.
- Generic industrial catalogs with crowded navigation, unexplained acronyms, tiny specifications, and competing calls to action.
- Apple imitation, black-and-orange cinematic machinery, or product-launch effects that obscure evaluation.
- Gamer RGB, neon gradients, esports aggression, or dashboard cosplay for SimMount.
- Endless identical card grids, promotional badge noise, urgency tricks, fake scarcity, unsupported reviews, and decorative metrics.
- Hiding compatibility, deposit terms, machine availability, shipping scope, or support limitations until late in the journey.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA across Thai and English. Preserve semantic landmarks, logical heading order, keyboard operation, visible focus, 44px minimum touch targets where practical, zoom-friendly layouts, descriptive alternative text, error association, and status announcements. Do not rely on color, hover, animation, or spatial position alone.

Motion is optional enhancement. Reduced-motion users receive the same information and complete states without scroll hijacking, hidden content, autoplay audio, or delayed access. Prices and machine deposits use Shopify market-aware money formatting; Thailand defaults to THB. User-facing dates use Asia/Bangkok when a local display is required, while stored timestamps remain UTC.
