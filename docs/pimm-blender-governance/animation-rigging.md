# PIMM Animation and Rigging

Machine-operation documents are the authority for named movable assemblies, permitted axes and limits, neutral pose, endpoint transforms, operating sequence, mold/platen/injection/pneumatic/controller relationships, and hose/cable behavior. Unknown mechanics and impossible movement block animation until the owner supplies or approves the missing information.

Scene-local rigs may control only approved animation controls. They must retain named controls and recorded endpoint contracts. Preflight verifies endpoint transforms and controller values before proof or final rendering. Static/poster and animation endpoints must match in release QA. Never invent an operation, assume an unstated transform, alter authoritative product geometry, or replace physical controller segments with text or image overlays.
